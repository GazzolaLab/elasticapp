* References
The following references are useful in understanding the scope of collision detection:

** General references:
These contain all and more information than needed. I would suggest doing them first to get a
good history of the subject (including GJK, EPA, broad and narrow phase, fine-detection tests
optimization etc.)
    1) C. Ericson. Real-time collision detection. CRC Press, 2004.
    2) G. Van Den Bergen. Collision detection in interactive 3D environments. CRC Press, 2003.
A more condensed view on the topic:
    1) https://www.toptal.com/game/video-game-physics-part-ii-collision-detection-for-solid-objects

** GJK and EPA:
    1) https://caseymuratori.com/blog_0003 (I should have seen this first, the best explanation of GJK I could find on the web)
    2) http://www.dyn4j.org/2010/04/gjk-gilbert-johnson-keerthi/
    3) http://vec3.ca/gjk/implementation/ : The only clear explanation of the possible cases in 3D
    4) http://www.dyn4j.org/2010/05/epa-expanding-polytope-algorithm/
    5) EPA in 3D : http://hacktank.net/blog/?p=119

** HHG
    1) F. Schornbaum. Hierarchical hash grids for coarse collision detection. Student Thesis, University of Erlangen-Nuremberg, 2009.
